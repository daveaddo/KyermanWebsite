About Kyerman

Made for your measure.

Kyerman by Koba creates handcrafted, made-to-measure suits designed around the individual. Every piece begins with your measurements, your preferences, and the way you want to wear it.

Your suit begins with you. Measurements are taken with attention to proportion, posture, and personal preference to create a fit that feels natural.

The Fabric
